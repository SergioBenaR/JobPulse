Reference route: three stops

A reference for exploring in your editor, not a record of a hands-on tour completed by the user.

  1. src/main.js — find function save. If writing fails, the offer stays pending and an error appears. On success, the view updates.
  2. src/storage.js — find commitEvaluation and resetDemo. The key is jobpulse.demo.v1; resetting only restores this key.
  3. src/model.js — find calculateMetrics. It counts votes and reasons; reason percentages use all rejections and allow multiple selections.

To change this, start here

Sample offers, salaries and votes

src/data.js: offers and seedEvaluations. Keep identifiers consistent and preserve the DEMO notice. Proper names and internal storage identifiers remain unchanged in the English version.

Offer card and rejection panel

src/review.js: renderReview, rejectionDialog and bindSwipe. Closing cancels; submitting saves details; skipping saves only the rejection.

Visible metrics and recommendations

src/dashboard.js > renderDashboard. Calculations live in src/model.js; comments are escaped using src/ui.js > escapeHTML.

Design and centering

src/styles.css: :root variables, .review-layout and responsive rules. The final layout removes sidebars and slogans, centers the card at a maximum of 510 px, and preserves fonts and sizes.

A practice to reuse

Turn an intention into an observable result before coding. prd.md > Panel empresarial requires real updates. tests/model.test.js checks exact vote and reason changes; tests/browser.mjs checks the full journey. Visual checks verify centering and one DEMO notice. In another project, first define what should change on screen, then verify that change.
